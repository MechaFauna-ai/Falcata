WHAT A NODE MEANS

A candidate is an experiment,
not a promise.

Workers explored separate code branches. The operator tested candidates, checked model identity and broader correctness, and sought independent review. A faster local kernel still had to improve a useful workload.

Each ratio belongs to its own campaign baseline and timing protocol. Outcome labels such as “improved” compare a candidate with its parent; the displayed ratio compares it with the campaign baseline. A candidate can beat the baseline and still regress from its parent.

Early replay scores, short probes, construction timings and full training are different measurements. They are never multiplied into a release speedup here. “Accepted” means eligible in that search; the article links separately identify integrated code.
